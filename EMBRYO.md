# Embryo Live Channel

Embryo Live Channel is Embryo-Zero's fork of [Owncast](https://github.com/owncast/owncast) (8fbc3df, commit 8fbc3df). It does self-hosted live channel with chat.

- Our changes live on the `embryo` branch. The original license files (LICENSE) are unchanged.
- Legal notes: LEGAL.md. Dated list of our changes: CHANGES-EMBRYO.md.
